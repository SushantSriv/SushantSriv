## 🧭 About me

<img align="right" width="380" src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/74038190/212749726-d36b8253-74bb-4509-870d-e29ed3b8ff4a.gif" alt="" />

<img src="https://flagcdn.com/24x18/no.png" width="20" alt="Norway" /> &nbsp;Based in **Oslo** since 2022, with **4+ years** of building end-to-end tech that actually ships.
I like the whole path — from a messy CSV to a model, to an API, to something a human can click.

- 🏗️ &nbsp;**IT Consultant @ Aibel** — internal tooling in **C#, WPF & PowerShell**, working shoulder to shoulder with engineers to automate the boring parts.
- 🤖 &nbsp;**Applied AI** — LLM apps, Whisper speech pipelines, and agentic patterns with **Semantic Kernel** + **Azure AI Foundry**.
- 📈 &nbsp;**Data science & MLOps** — predictive models and time-series in **scikit-learn / PyTorch**, shipped through **FastAPI, Docker & CI/CD**.
- 📊 &nbsp;**BI & storytelling** — **Power BI**, Streamlit and Jupyter turned into dashboards people actually reopen.
- 🌐 &nbsp;**Fullstack** — **React + Vite** front-ends, Leaflet maps, real-time APIs on FastAPI and Node.
- 🧪 &nbsp;**Right now** — agentic orchestration: LLMs, plugins, APIs and workflows wired into one coherent system.

🏃 &nbsp;Off-keyboard: trail running, lifting, a tennis court, or eating my way through a new cuisine.
